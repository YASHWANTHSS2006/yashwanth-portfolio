Forecasting

Retail Sales Forecasting

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn

Four-week-ahead sales forecasts for 45 Walmart stores, validated with walk-forward testing so every forecast uses only past data.

  • Error reduced by 26% against a seasonal baseline (WMAPE 3.81% vs 5.13%)
  • Leakage-safe lag and rolling features on 6,435 weekly records
  • Ridge, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting compared head to head
  • Stores segmented into 3 groups with KMeans
